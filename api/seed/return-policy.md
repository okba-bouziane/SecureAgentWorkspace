# Return Policy

> Sample data: a fictional shop used to demonstrate the Secure Agent Workspace. Not a real policy.

## Return window

Customers can return most products within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the day the order reaches the status `delivered`. Orders that are still `pending`, `paid` or `shipped` cannot be returned yet, but they can be cancelled.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused and in the original packaging with all accessories. Opened software, custom-built products and gift cards are not returnable. Items with visible damage caused by the customer are refused and sent back at the customer's cost.

## Refunds

Once a returned item passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Shipping costs are refunded only when the item arrived damaged or was not what the customer ordered.

## Defective products

Electronics such as keyboards, monitors, headphones and webcams have a 12 month warranty. A defective item is replaced or refunded without a return fee. Support needs the order id and a short description of the defect.

## Cancellations

An order can be cancelled for free while its status is `pending` or `paid`. After it is `shipped`, the customer has to wait for delivery and use the normal return window. A cancelled order has the status `cancelled` and the payment is refunded automatically.
