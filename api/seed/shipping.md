# Shipping Guide

> Sample data: a fictional shop used to demonstrate the Secure Agent Workspace. Not a real carrier policy.

## Delivery options

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat 9.90 EUR. Orders above 100 EUR ship free with the standard option.

## Dispatch times

Orders paid before 14:00 on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders paid later leave the next business day. When an order leaves the warehouse its status changes from `paid` to `shipped` and the customer receives a tracking number.

## Tracking

Tracking numbers become active about 12 hours after dispatch. If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, support opens an investigation with the carrier.

## Large items

Standing desks, ergonomic chairs and monitors above 27 inches ship on a pallet. Delivery needs a phone call to agree on a time slot and takes 5 to 8 business days.

## Lost parcels

If a parcel is not delivered 10 business days after dispatch it is considered lost. The shop then ships a replacement or refunds the customer, whichever the customer prefers.
