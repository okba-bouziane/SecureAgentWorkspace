## Warranty handling for mechanical keyboards

The keyboard supplier covers switch failures for 24 months. Customers who report a failed key switch should send the order id and a photo. Replacement keyboards ship from the supplier within 7 business days.

Keycaps, cables and wrist rests are not covered by the supplier warranty. Return shipping for warranty cases is paid by the supplier.
